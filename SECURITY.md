# Security

If you have found a weakness in anything published by this organisation, we would like to hear it from you first, and in private.

## How to report

**Please do not open a public issue.**

1. **Preferred:** on the repository concerned, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. This reaches the maintainers privately.
2. **If that is not offered:** write to [contact@bit-trading-company.com](mailto:contact@bit-trading-company.com) with the subject line `SECURITY`. Send only enough to show the problem exists. We will arrange a safer channel for the rest.

## What helps

- The repository, and the version or commit
- What an attacker could do, in a sentence
- The steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- Whether anyone else knows

## What to expect

A person reads every report and will tell you when they have. We do not publish a response time, because we would rather make no promise than break one. We will keep you informed as the matter is understood and fixed, and we will credit you when it is disclosed, if you wish.

There is no bounty at present.

## What we ask

- Give us a reasonable time to fix the matter before you speak of it publicly.
- Touch only your own accounts and data. If you meet someone else's, stop, and tell us.
- Do not degrade a service, and do not use what you find for anything except showing that it exists.

## Scope

The repositories of this organisation and the services they run. A weakness in someone else's software belongs with its maintainers, though we are glad to be told when it affects us.
