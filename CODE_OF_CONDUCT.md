# Conduct

These repositories are places of work. Anyone who comes to them in good faith should be able to work in them without being made smaller for it.

## What we expect

- **Argue with the work, not the person.** Be as exact as you like about what is wrong with a change, and say nothing about who made it.
- **Assume a reason.** Most odd decisions had one. Ask before you correct.
- **Say what you know and how you know it.** Mark a guess as a guess.
- **Leave room.** For the newcomer, for the person writing in a second language, for the one who disagrees with you.
- **Say what you are.** If a message was written by software, it says so.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, in any form, of anyone.
- Insults, slurs, or remarks about a person's body, origin, identity, belief or circumstances.
- Sexual language or attention that was not invited.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Persisting after being asked to stop.

## Where this applies

In every repository, issue, pull request and discussion of the organisation, and anywhere else a person is acting on its behalf.

## If something goes wrong

Write to [contact@bit-trading-company.com](mailto:contact@bit-trading-company.com). A person reads it. Say what happened and where; a link is enough. Your report is kept in confidence.

What follows depends on what happened: a word in private, a removed comment, a temporary ban, a permanent one. Judgements about who belongs here are made by a person and never by software.
